AI Beer Counter

AI Beer Counter is an AI or video app made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Listed on qaimos.co.uk among the Qaimos iOS apps.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/#portfolio

From the store listing

AI beer counter is a quiet beer journal for people who said “just the one.”

She doesn’t need the running total. You might. Don’t tell her the exact number — tell Pintlog. One tap logs the pint and the price. What happens after closing time lives here, not in the joint bank app.

HOW IT WORKS Tap the glass. That’s a drink at your usual price. Miss-tap? Undo sits there for a few seconds, which is longer than most alibis.

A mate got this one? Shared · free. It still counts as a beer. It never counts as spend. The till stays innocent. The glass does not.

TODAY The big number is today. Under it: this week’s logged drinks, what you actually paid, and the quiet days you left alone. Optional weekly intention, if you like a gentle fence. No streaks. No shame. No medals for dryness or for damage.

DAYS A calendar of when you drank. Marked days are the evidence. Tap any evening — including last Thursday, which you have already rewritten in your head — to add or delete a pint.

SPENT The unofficial household secret: a till in every currency you really paid. Pounds, dollars, baht, pesos, dirhams, and the rest. See each one as logged, then a converted total in the currency you pick.

The beer line is the trend. Height is spend. Each little glass fills with how many you had that day.
