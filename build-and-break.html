Build & Break

Build & Break is a mobile game made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. A calm topple puzzle: tap blocks, drop weights, set off TNT — and land every gem on the ground in as few taps as possible.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/buildbreak/

From the store listing

Description (max 4000):

Build & Break is a calm, satisfying topple puzzle. Each level is a little tower of wood, stone, glass and steel with gems resting on top. Your job: bring every gem down to the ground in as few taps as you can.

How To Play

  • Tap a block to break it. Everything above it drops.
  • Wood breaks in one tap. Stone takes two. Glass shatters when anything lands on it.
  • Steel can't be broken — knock out what's holding it up instead.
  • TNT blasts every breakable block around it, and sets off other TNT.
  • Build: drop a weight into any column. A long fall crushes wood, cracks stone and sets off TNT — and it doesn't cost a tap.
  • Get every gem onto the ground to clear the level.

Why You'Ll Like It

  • One clear rule set, lots of clever chain reactions
  • Stars for finishing at or under par — replay any level to improve
  • Five short tutorial levels, then fresh procedurally built towers
  • Hint button when you're stuck
  • No timer, no pressure, no account
  • Plays offline — progress is saved on your device

Ads &Amp; Purchases

Free to play with a small banner from level 5 and an optional rewarded video every few levels (you can always skip it). A single one-time purchase removes ads.
