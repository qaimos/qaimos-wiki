CalScai

CalScai is an AI or video app made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. CalScai: log meals from photos, track calories and hydration on your iPhone. Optional CalScai Pro subscription for unlimited Snap analyses.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/calscai/

From the store listing

Your plate. Your story. One gorgeous screen.

CalScai is the unapologetically simple way to flirt with better habits: snap what you ate, watch Today light up, and keep the vibe honest without a spreadsheet meltdown.

The Hero Frame

Frame the food. Hit analyze. Your photo slips out over encrypted HTTPS, then comes back dressed in calories, protein, carbs, fat, and a cheeky hydration hint. Every successful scan spends one credit, because good data should feel earned, not endless.

Today That Slaps

A calorie ring that actually turns heads. Macros laid out clean. Hydration bar edging toward your target. A calendar you can tap through like vinyl: any day, instant truth. Meals stack in a tight list. Swipe one away when reality edits itself.

Goals you can crank up or dial down. Manual calories when you demolished something without a photo. Water logging when your bottle did the heavy lifting. Save, and Today updates like it missed you.

Credits That Behave

You land with credits in the bank. Need more heat? In app purchases top you up through Apple: no account circus inside the app, just you and the receipt.

Your Secret Diary

Everything deliciously local: meals, tweaks, goals, balance, on this iPhone, inside a rolling window that trims the past so storage never ghosts you.

The Fine Print Still Hot

iPhone. Internet when you analyze. Lighting and angles still matter: estimates wink, they do not x ray your soul.

Not Medical Advice

CalScai whispers nutrition guesses, not prescriptions. For clinical decisions, bring a pro, not a pixel.
