Cliptum

Cliptum is an AI or video app made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Cliptum turns your photos and videos into polished 9:16 portrait reels on iPhone.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/cliptum/

From the store listing

Create polished portrait reels from your favourite photos and videos with Cliptum.

Choose 2–10 clips from your photo library or capture something new with the camera. Arrange your moments, select a template, preview the result, and save your finished 9:16 reel directly to Photos.

Features:

  • Combine photos and videos
  • Create portrait 9:16 reels
  • Choose from multiple visual templates
  • Use Plain mode for clean cuts without effects
  • Preview your reel before exporting
  • Export in 1080 × 1920 resolution
  • Capture new photos and videos in the app
  • Media processing stays on your device

Cliptum keeps reel creation simple — without a complicated editing timeline.
