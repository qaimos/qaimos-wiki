CocktailMeAI

CocktailMeAI is an AI or video app made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Listed on qaimos.co.uk among the Qaimos AI apps for iOS and Android.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/#portfolio

From the store listing

CocktailMeAI turns what’s on your shelf into cocktails and mocktails you can make tonight.

Type what you have, speak your ingredients, or snap up to 3 photos of your bottles — our AI bartender finds drinks you can mix right now, suggests what to buy next, and shares full recipes in millilitres or ounces.

How It Works

  • Type — List spirits, mixers, juice, soda, and anything drinkable
  • Speak — Dictate what’s in your bar hands-free
  • Photo — Pick photos of your bottles; AI detects ingredients
  • Results — Makeable drinks, Bartender AI advice, and missing-ingredient tips
  • Share — Send drink ideas to friends or coworkers with a link
  • My Bar — Revisit your last 40 lookups anytime

Features

  • Cocktails and mocktails from what you already have
  • Recipes with pour amounts in ml or oz (your choice in Settings)
  • 9 languages: English, Portuguese, French, German, Spanish, Chinese, Arabic, and more
  • Simple tab navigation: Bar AI, history, and Settings

FREE TIER Free users receive one AI analysis per calendar week (type, speak, or photo — shared weekly limit).
