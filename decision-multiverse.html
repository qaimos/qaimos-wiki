Decision Multiverse

Decision Multiverse is an AI or video app made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Listed on qaimos.co.uk among the Qaimos AI apps; the Android section describes AI apps "for decisions, nutrition, and short-form video".

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/#portfolio

From the store listing

WHAT IF YOUR TIMELINES WERE VISIBLE?

Decision Multiverse is a private journal for choices - not a crystal ball. Record a decision, add a little context, and explore alternate storylines: the branch you took, and the paths you didn’t. Every replay is a mirror, not a forecast.

Timelines

  • Add decisions with clear options and mark your preferred path
  • Scroll your history and open any moment
  • Swipe to remove or clean up from detail—your data stays in your control on device

Replay &Amp; Branches

  • Pick an alternate “if I had chosen this…” fork and read three branches—tone, headline, narrative, and ripple bullets
  • Saved AI branches come back when you return; refresh only when you want something new

BRANCH AI & AI DEEP MIND (OPTIONAL)

  • Turn on AI Deep Mind to generate richer branches through a secure hosted endpoint - no API keys stored on your phone for the default setup
  • Networks fail sometimes; the app falls back to polished on-device templates so you’re never stuck staring at a blank screen
  • SwiftData keeps your timelines on device; cached AI branch text stays local after generation.

Designed To Feel Calm

Soft cream, blush, and espresso tones with serif titles - sci-fi mood without harsh chrome.

For Reflection Only

These branches are for introspection and journaling - not medical, legal, or financial advice, and not predictions about real outcomes.

Start with one decision you keep replaying in your head - give it a timeline, then walk the multiverse.
