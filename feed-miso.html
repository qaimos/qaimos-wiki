Feed Miso

Feed Miso is a mobile game made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Listed on qaimos.co.uk among the Qaimos Android games.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/#portfolio

From the store listing

Miso is hungry. The koi hangs from fishing line. You have fifteen seconds and one finger.

Slice silk. Pop bubbles. Dodge urchins. Tilt your phone to swing the fish while it is still on the rope. Drop dinner into the cat.

150 ponds. Three stars each. No lives. No energy bar. Just pond physics and a ticking clock.

Feed Miso is free to play. Banner ads may appear. Optional Remove Ads purchase hides banners. Progress stays on your device — no account required.
