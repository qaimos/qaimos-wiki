Gears & Flow

Gears & Flow is a mobile game made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Tap gears to connect pipes, release colored flow, and fill matching bins before time runs out.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/gearsflow/

From the store listing

Gears & Flow is an industrial puzzle game. Rotate gears to connect pipes, then release colored flow into matching bins before time runs out.

How To Play

  • Tap any gear to rotate it 90°
  • Align gear teeth so colors can travel between tiles
  • Press Release flow to start the simulation
  • Route every color into its matching bin to win
  • Wrong bin, blocked path, or timeout means you lose

Endless Challenge

  • Tutorial levels teach the mechanics step by step
  • Procedurally generated puzzles keep difficulty rising
  • Earn up to 3 stars per level — fewer rotations and faster wins score higher

Relaxing But Tricky

Clean industrial visuals, smooth tap controls, and bite-sized levels make Gears & Flow perfect for quick breaks — while tighter layouts and mixed colors keep experts planning ahead.

Ads

Gears & Flow is free to download and play. The app displays advertisements through Google AdMob:

  • Banner ads appear at the bottom of the screen during gameplay from level 5 onward
  • Optional rewarded video ads may be offered every 6 levels from level 10 onward — watching is always voluntary and can be skipped
  • A one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase permanently removes all banner and rewarded ads

Gears & Flow does not require an account or login. Level progress, star ratings, and ad-removal status are stored locally on your device only.

Third-party services used by the app may collect limited data:

  • Google AdMob — device identifiers and ad interaction data for advertising and analytics
  • Apple App Store — purchase processing if you buy Remove Ads (we receive purchase confirmation, not payment card details)
