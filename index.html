Qaimos LTD

Qaimos LTD is a UK registered limited company building agentic AI, mobile games and apps for iOS and Android, and Shopify apps — 20 live products across the App Store, Google Play and Shopify, according to qaimos.co.uk.

The studio describes itself as "a UK product studio shipping AI-native games and apps on every major store": casual mobile games for iOS and Android, AI apps people use every day, and Shopify apps for merchants, built by the same agentic AI practice it offers to clients. It runs a portfolio model: ship many focused products quickly, measure them, and put capital behind the ones that find traction.

Beyond its own products, Qaimos offers agentic AI consulting: consult with Qaimos. Contact: info@qaimos.co.uk.
