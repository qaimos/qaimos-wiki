Ledge Tower

Ledge Tower is a mobile game made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Listed on qaimos.co.uk among the Qaimos games for iOS and Android.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/#portfolio

From the store listing

A block slides over the ledge. One tap drops it. Keep the overlap. Miss, and the tower falls.

Ledge Tower is a one-tap arcade from Qaimos Gaming. Line up the moving slab, cut it clean, and climb 1,000 levels. Flush landings score more. Precision on a clear is rated from 1 to 5 stars.

How To Play

  • Watch the block travel over the stack
  • Tap when it lines up
  • The overlapping slice stays — the rest falls
  • Hit the stack count to clear the level
  • Tilt the device to orbit the tower

Inside The Game

  • 1,000 levels
  • Neon slabs and changing skies
  • Combos for edge-to-edge drops
  • Built for iPhone and iPad
  • No account. Progress stays on your device
