LuckyNum

LuckyNum is an AI or video app made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Listed on qaimos.co.uk among the Qaimos Android apps.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/#portfolio

From the store listing

LuckyNum turns picking numbers into a small ritual — calm, cinematic, and made to feel special.

Draw daily luck, classic lucky sets, or lottery-style picks with a staggered ball reveal and crisp haptics. Each draw lands with presence, not a cold random dump.

What you can do

  • Daily Lucky — one number for today
  • Lucky Six & Quick Pick — classic free sets
  • Powerball, Mega Millions & EuroMillions presets (Pro)
  • Save and revisit your draw history
  • Customize the moment — tap Draw and watch the reveal unfold

Free

  • 3 draws per day
  • Core presets
  • Recent history

LuckyNum Pro • Unlimited draws

  • Full lottery presets
  • Complete draw history

Whether you’re choosing lottery numbers, picking a lucky set for the day, or just enjoying the draw — LuckyNum makes chance feel intentional.
