PostcardsSnap

PostcardsSnap is a mobile game made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Listed on qaimos.co.uk among the Qaimos iOS games.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/#portfolio

From the store listing

Postcard Snap is a relaxing swap-and-snap photo puzzle. Every level is a postcard from somewhere beautiful, cut into cards and shuffled. Drag cards to swap them — when neighbours that belong together meet, they SNAP and move as one. Put the picture back together, earn stars and stick it in your album.

How To Play

  • Drag any card onto another to swap them.
  • Cards that belong together snap and then move as a group.
  • Line up several at once for a chain snap!
  • Finish in fewer moves for three stars.

Travel The World

  • A world tour of 60 real postcard photos — London, Paris, Venice, Mykonos, Kyoto, the Grand Canyon and more.
  • Ten postcards per country — stamp every leg of your passport, then start the next Grand Tour.
  • Challenge postcards test you with a move limit.

Come Back Every Day

  • A new Postcard of the Day, the same for everyone.
  • Keep your daily streak going — every 7th day gives 3 bonus hints and a gold stamp.

Relaxed And Fair

  • No timer and no lives — play at your own pace.
  • Free Peek on every level to see the whole picture.
  • Hints place a card for you when you're stuck.
  • Plays offline — no account needed.
  • Save your favourite postcards to share or use as a wallpaper.

Ads &Amp; Purchases

Free to play with a small banner from level 4 and an occasional full-screen ad between levels (from level 6, at most every few levels). Videos are always optional and only shown when you ask for an extra hint, peek, moves or a streak save. A single one-time purchase removes banner and between-level ads.
