Reelsium

Reelsium is an AI or video app made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Reelsium helps you make scroll-stopping Reels, Stories, and Posts — templates, looks, text colours, then export and share.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/reelsium/

From the store listing

Reelsium is your pocket studio for short video and feed photos. Pick a Reel, Story, or Post, add photos or videos, choose a look and text color, then export and share.

What You Get

  • One-tap starters for Reel, Story, and Post
  • Creator mode for photos and videos
  • On-screen hooks with selectable text colors
  • Looks that feel feed-native
  • Export video or photo — post in the apps you already use

Free To Start

  • 3 exports per day
  • Core templates and looks

Reelsium Pro

  • Unlimited exports
  • Pro templates and looks

What Reelsium Is Not

Reelsium does not buy followers, automate engagement, or log into your social accounts. Create in Reelsium. Post where you already create.
