Roast You

Roast You is an AI or video app made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Upload a selfie or fit, get an AI roast or backhanded compliment, and share a card on social.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/roastyou/

From the store listing

You have a photo worth reacting to — a selfie, an outfit, a room — but turning it into something funny enough to post takes time, wit, and energy you don’t always have. Generic captions fall flat. Asking friends can feel awkward. You want something sharp, shareable, and instant — not another boring filter.

Roast You fixes that. Upload any pic, pick a roast style, and get a custom AI line in seconds — formatted as a card ready for stories, group chats, and posts.

How it works

  • Upload a selfie, fit, room, or anything roastable.
  • Choose your poison: Savage Roast, Backhanded Compliment, or Chaotic Hype.
  • Tap Roast You — AI reads your photo and writes a one-of-a-kind roast.
  • Share the card or copy the caption with hashtags built in.

Credits

  • 1 successful AI roast = 1 credit
  • Start with free credits
  • Need more? Buy a 10-credit pack in the app via Apple In-App Purchase

Why people use it

  • Instant dopamine — no brainstorming captions
  • Meme-ready share cards, not plain text
  • Three roast modes for different vibes
  • No account required — open, upload, roast, share

Powered by qaimos.co.uk AI — your image is sent securely for vision analysis; results come back fast and stay on your device until you share them.

Good to know Roast You is entertainment, not advice. AI roasts are jokes for sharing — keep it playful and don’t use generated content to harass anyone.
