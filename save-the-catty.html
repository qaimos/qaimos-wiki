Save the Catty

Save the Catty is a mobile game made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Save the catty — comedy maze escape.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/savethecatty/

From the store listing

Catty got left in the car. The family took the bags, the keys, the snacks… and somehow forgot one very important passenger.

Now it’s your job to get that cat out.

Save the catty is a comedy maze escape: sneak through cluttered cabins, zombie yards, garages, and stranger places beyond — while rats skitter, zombies roam, and traps wait for a clumsy paw.

How To Play

  • Steer Catty with the left stick
  • Collect coins for bonus ammo
  • Aim and fire the minigun when you’ve earned (or bought) rounds
  • Reach the exit before noise, shocks, or a bad surprise ends the run

Features

  • 19 handcrafted escape levels
  • Coins, spikes, shocks, and escalating chaos
  • Persistent minigun ammo across levels
  • Daily lives and “two strikes” level drama
  • Silly story, smug cat energy, and a proper thank-you ending

Grab the coins. Survive the maze. Save the catty.
