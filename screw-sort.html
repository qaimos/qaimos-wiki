Screw Sort

Screw Sort is a mobile game made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. Screw Sort is a free iOS and Android puzzle game by Qaimos. Tap screws from stacks into a tray and match three same colors in a row to clear. Endless levels, star ratings, offline play. Available on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/screwsort/

From the store listing

Screw Sort is a satisfying screw-stacking puzzle game. Tap screws from the top of each stack, fill the tray, and clear groups of three matching colors — but only when they land in a row!

How To Play

  • Tap the top screw on any stack
  • Screws move into your tray one by one
  • Three same colors in a row disappear from the tray
  • Scattered matches do NOT clear — plan your order carefully
  • Clear every screw to win the level
  • Fill the tray with no row of three and you lose

Endless Challenge

  • Hundreds of procedurally generated levels
  • Difficulty ramps as you progress
  • Earn up to 3 stars per level — fewer moves means more stars
  • Unlock new level packs as you advance

Relaxing But Tricky

Bright colors, smooth controls, and bite-sized levels make Screw Sort perfect for quick breaks — while deeper stacks and tighter trays keep experts guessing.

Ads

Screw Sort is free to download and play. The app displays advertisements through Google AdMob:

  • Banner ads appear at the bottom of the screen during gameplay from level 6 onward
  • Optional rewarded video ads may be offered on scheduled levels from level 11 onward — watching is always voluntary and can be skipped
  • A one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase permanently removes all banner and rewarded ads

Screw Sort does not require an account or login. Level progress, star ratings, and ad-removal status are stored locally on your device only.

Third-party services used by the app may collect limited data:

  • Google AdMob — device identifiers and ad interaction data for advertising and analytics
  • Apple App Store — purchase processing if you buy Remove Ads (we receive purchase confirmation, not payment card details)
