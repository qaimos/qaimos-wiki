TierBar

TierBar is a Shopify app made by Qaimos LTD, a UK product studio. TierBar — quantity break tiers on product pages with automatic checkout discounts.

Official page: https://qaimos.co.uk/tierbar/

From the store listing

TierBar lets merchants set up quantity break offers in a few clicks from Shopify Admin. Merchants select a product, define tiers, and add the theme block; discounts apply automatically at checkout. On qaimos.co.uk the Qaimos Shopify apps are described as B2B apps that help merchants lift order value and stay compliant.
